Get to know me

About Me

I am a final-year Decision Mathematics and Data Analytics student at Universiti Utara Malaysia (CGPA 3.71) who turns messy, real-world data into decisions people can act on. For my final-year project, I compared six machine-learning models to predict telecom customer churn at 80% accuracy and deployed the best one as a live dashboard for non-technical users. During my industrial training, I handled the data architecture and data engineering for an automated pipeline in Microsoft Fabric that turns raw web data into a Power BI dashboard used by the business team. My strengths are Python and SQL, dimensional data modelling, and translating technical analysis into clear business insight, communicated confidently in English, Malay, and Chinese.

Beyond the numbers, I come from a background that blends analytical thinking and creative expression. Alongside my degree I hold a Minor in Information Technology, and outside of academics I was a member of the UUM Convocation Orchestra, led event coordination roles in large-scale university concerts and cultural events, and have actively contributed to university life through leadership and organisational responsibilities.

I enjoy solving real-world problems using data, and I believe the best solutions are not only accurate but also accessible to the people who need them.

I am someone who takes ownership of my work. Whether it is writing a full academic report, building a Python pipeline, or coordinating a 200-person concert event, I approach every task with attention to detail and a drive to see it through. I am a trilingual communicator comfortable in English, Malay, and Chinese.

Why I Chose This Topic

I chose this topic because I wanted to work on a real-world business problem with a clear and measurable impact. Customer churn affects millions of dollars in revenue every year, yet many companies still rely on guesswork to identify at-risk customers. What motivated me even more was the accessibility gap. Most existing tools require advanced programming knowledge, which means business managers cannot use them. I wanted to close that gap by deploying the model as a dashboard that anyone can use without writing a single line of code.
